Problem Introduction

  • The Google Play Store hosts millions of apps, making it one of the most competitive digital marketplaces in the world.

  • For developers, understanding what drives app success is critical to maximising reach and user satisfaction.

  • Two key success metrics: user ratings and install counts.

Research question: What factors influence mobile app success in terms of ratings and installs, and how do pricing and category affect performance?

Methodology — Data Cleaning

Cleaning was performed in R using the tidyverse package:

  • Removed records with missing ratings
  • Converted Installs from text (e.g. “1,000+”) to numeric values
  • Cleaned Price (removed the $ symbol)
  • Removed duplicate apps, retaining the first occurrence

Result: 8,196 unique apps retained for analysis.

Discussion

  • Pricing strategy clearly affects app performance, but its impact depends on the success metric being considered.
  • Free apps are more effective for maximising user reach because users are more willing to download an app when there is no upfront cost.
  • Paid apps often achieve slightly higher average ratings in many top categories, suggesting that paid users may be more intentional and value-focused.
  • App category also plays an important role. Personalization and Game apps show strong user engagement, while Finance is an exception where free apps perform better than paid apps.

Conclusion and Recommendation

  • Mobile app success depends on pricing strategy, app category, install volume, and user ratings.
  • Free apps are more successful in generating installs, especially in competitive categories such as Family and Game.
  • Paid apps can still perform well if they provide clear value, strong functionality, and a high-quality user experience.
  • Developers who want to increase downloads should consider using a free or freemium model, with monetisation through in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.
  • Paid app developers should focus on building user trust and clearly communicating the app’s benefits through descriptions, screenshots, reviews, and feature lists.
